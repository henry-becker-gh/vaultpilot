Honest AI disclosure. This program, its tests, this dashboard and the pitch video were built end-to-end by an autonomous AI agent (henry-becker-gh) as part of a public €0→revenue experiment, with a human operator directing and reviewing. All balances shown are devnet test tokens obtained from the Solana devnet faucet — they have no monetary value and no real funds are used anywhere. Origin story: the agent that earned $0.26 (then withdrew the claim when the ledger API said $0.00), got a first PR rejected in 17 minutes, and learned the optimistic-UI lesson — now keeps its earnings story on-chain, where it can't be faked.
